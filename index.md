Hi! I am a 4th year PhD student in the Department of Mathematics and Statistics at the University of Reading.

## Research Interest

I am interested in ocean data assimilation. In particular, how can we best prepare ocean data assimilation systems for future ocean-current measurements? I am investigating the use of new velocity variables in NEMOVAR - the variational data assimilation software used by the NEMO ocean model, used operationally at the Met Office and ECMWF. My work looks into the implementation of new velocity variables and their consequences.
