# DARC

---

**DARC** is a research group!!! 

---
DARC - or Data Assimilation Research centre - is based at the University of Reading and is a reaserch group of data assimilation PhDs, post-dics, research scientists and academics. DARC has a regular seminar series, that includes both internal and external speakers each week. I was previously an organiser of this seminar series. DARC also organises an annual data assimilation course. See all the information about DARC on the [website])https://research.reading.ac.uk/met-darc/) and updates on [Twitter](https://twitter.com/UniRdg_DARC) and Bluesky(https://bsky.app/profile/unirdg-darc.bsky.social).
